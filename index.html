💡 Tips for grown-ups (ages 8–9)

  • Concrete → pictures → numbers. Build 3×4 with Lego first, then look at the dot pictures here, then do the sum in your head.
  • Second chance, then explain. A wrong answer gets a hint and one retry (half points) before the answer is shown. Mistakes are practice, not failure.
  • Echo reading. You read one sentence aloud, your child repeats it. Then click unknown words together.
  • Draw the bar model. For every word problem draw one bar per amount, then ask: which part is missing? Pictures first, numbers second.
  • Short and daily. 15 minutes beats an hour on Sunday. The streak counter rewards coming back.
  • Say it out loud. "I take 1 ten from the 5 tens…" – talking through steps shows what they really understand.
  • Real life. Shop totals, sharing pizza slices, recipe doubling, counting stairs.
  • Praise effort ("you tried a new way!") rather than speed.
